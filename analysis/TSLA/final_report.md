## AGREEMENTS
- **Valuation is extreme**: Both analysts acknowledge 401x trailing P/E and $1.6T market cap far exceeds current earnings power. No dispute on the numbers.
- **Profitability is deteriorating**: FY2025 net income down 47%, Q4 2025 EPS down 64% YoY, operating margins at 5.1% (from 17% in 2022).
- **CAN SLIM score is poor (2/5)**: Both note the stock fails on earnings quality, annual growth, and proximity to highs.
- **Elon Musk's $1B purchase is notable**: Both recognize the CEO's personal investment at ~$380 as a genuine positive signal of insider conviction.
- **Mean reversion indicators are fully neutral**: RSI 48, Z-score 0.10 — no overbought or oversold pressures.

## DISAGREEMENTS
- **Value Analyst (SELL 7/10)**: The Weinstein Stage upgrade is a price-momentum signal carrying zero fundamental information. No new earnings data, no margin improvement, no Robotaxi revenue disclosure. The 3.4% price increase only makes the 401x P/E more stretched. Three fundamental frameworks (Graham, Damodaran, Lynch) independently produce SELL with strong conviction. The market prices in 30-35% annual earnings growth for a decade when sustainable growth is 10.4% and revenue is actually declining.
- **Technical Analyst (BUY 5/10)**: The Stage 2 reclassification is the most important technical signal. The stock is above a rising 30-week MA with proper MA alignment (150d and 200d both rising). Four of six frameworks improved since Feb 7. OBV crossed above its 20-day MA. Bollinger squeeze at 17th percentile signals an imminent move likely to resolve with the trend (upward). However, confidence is low due to contracting volume, fragile 3.7% cushion above 30-week MA, and CAN SLIM failure.

## CHANGE CONDITIONS
- **To upgrade to BUY (fundamental trigger)**: Trailing P/E compresses below 100 through earnings acceleration showing 2+ consecutive quarters of >50% YoY EPS growth, OR Robotaxi/FSD recurring revenue reaches $5B+ annual run rate with >50% gross margins.
- **To upgrade to BUY (technical trigger)**: Price holds above 30-week MA ($410), volume expands on rallies, 3-month RS vs SPX crosses above 1.0, and stock approaches the 50-day MA ($444) without failing.
- **To confirm SELL (technical deterioration)**: Weekly close below 30-week MA ($410) for two consecutive weeks, OR 6-month RS vs SPX falls below 1.0, OR OBV develops bearish divergence.

## RISK CONSIDERATIONS
- **Stage 2 is fragile**: Only 3.7% above the 30-week MA ($410). TSLA's daily ATR of $15.79 means a single bad day could test this level. Two weeks below $410 would invalidate the stage upgrade.
- **Bollinger squeeze resolution**: BB width at 17th percentile implies an imminent large move. If it resolves downward, $399 (lower band) and $383 (200-day MA) are the next support levels.
- **Earnings trajectory is negative**: Q4 2025 EPS $0.24 was -64% vs Q4 2024. Forward P/E of 151.5x requires massive EPS recovery not yet visible in quarterly data.
- **50-day MA resistance at $444**: Significant overhead resistance. Failure there would create a lower-high pattern strengthening the bear case.
- **Speculative optionality**: Robotaxi (launched June 2025), Optimus, FSD, and energy storage represent genuine but unproven catalysts. Six new production lines ramping in 2026. These could materially change the fundamental picture, but timing and magnitude are uncertain.
