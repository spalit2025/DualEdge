### A. WEINSTEIN STAGE ANALYSIS

- **30-Week MA Slope**: Rising
- **Price vs 30-Week MA**: Above ($425.21 vs $410.03)
- **Price-to-MA Ratio**: 1.037 (3.7% above)
- **Volume Pattern**: Contracting
- **Stage Classification**: Stage 2 (Advancing)
- **Confidence**: Moderate

**Analysis:**

The Stage 2 reclassification is technically valid but requires careful qualification. The key positives: the 30-week MA ($410.03) has a rising slope, and price ($425.21) has moved back above it after the sharp early-February selloff to $387.53 (intraday low on Feb 5). The price-to-MA ratio of 1.037 represents a meaningful improvement from the razor-thin 1.0105 reading on February 7, when the stock was sitting directly on the MA. The stock now has a 3.7% cushion above the key Weinstein level.

The critical concern is the **contracting volume pattern**. Classical Weinstein methodology expects Stage 2 advances to be confirmed by expanding volume on rallies and contracting volume on pullbacks. Examining the daily OHLCV data:

- **February 4-5 selloff** (to $406/$397): Volume was 74.6M and 72.8M -- elevated selling pressure
- **February 6-10 recovery** (to $411/$417/$425): Volume was 62.7M, 54.5M, 64.3M -- declining buy-side participation

Volume is declining as price rises. This is not textbook Stage 2 confirmation. A genuine Stage 2 breakout would show volume expanding on the recovery, signaling institutional commitment to the advance. The absence of that pattern is a yellow flag.

Additionally, the broader price structure matters. TSLA peaked at $489.88 on December 16, then declined 21% to the February 5 low of $387.53 (using intraday low). This pattern -- a sharp rally from September through mid-December followed by a multi-week corrective decline and a bounce off the 30-week MA -- could be interpreted two ways:

1. **Bullish interpretation**: A normal Stage 2 pullback to the 30-week MA, which is acting as support exactly as Weinstein predicts. The bounce off the MA is a buying opportunity within the context of an ongoing advance.
2. **Bearish interpretation**: A Stage 3 distribution top with a retest of the 30-week MA. The bounce is a dead-cat rally within a topping process that will eventually resolve lower.

The data supports the bullish interpretation slightly more than the bearish one, because: (a) the 30-week MA slope is still rising (not flat or rolling over, which would be classic Stage 3), (b) the 150-day and 200-day MAs are both rising and below the 30-week MA (proper bullish alignment), and (c) price has now put 3.7% of distance above the MA, not just sitting on it.

However, the moderate confidence is appropriate. I would characterize this as a **Stage 2 re-entry attempt** rather than a confirmed Stage 2 advance. The next 2-3 weeks of price action around the 30-week MA will be decisive.

**Weinstein Signal: CAUTIOUSLY BULLISH** -- Stage 2 classification is valid on the metrics but quality is undermined by contracting volume and proximity to a distribution pattern.
