## AGREEMENTS
- **Wide economic moat**: Both analysts agree Uber has a wide moat built on network effects (30M Uber One members, 2.2x cross-platform engagement), scale economies (70+ countries), and brand power.
- **FCF trajectory is impressive**: Both acknowledge free cash flow nearly doubled from $3.36B to $6.90B, and the capital-light model (0.55% capex/revenue) is a genuine competitive advantage.
- **Regulatory risk is the key long-term threat**: Both identify potential driver reclassification as the existential risk, though it has not materialized.
- **Insider selling is programmatic**: Both note the selling pattern appears tied to RSU vesting and pre-planned programs, not discretionary bearish signals.

## DISAGREEMENTS
- **Value Analyst (BUY 7/10)**: Wide moat with PEG 0.70-1.16, FCF doubling YoY, 42% upside to analyst consensus ($105), and 152% operating income growth on 18% revenue growth. The 26% pullback from highs creates a compelling entry point. The CAN SLIM "C" failure (-95.6% EPS decline) is distorted by one-time items in Q4 2024 ($6B deferred tax benefit), not operational deterioration.
- **Technical Analyst (SELL 7/10)**: Stage 4 decline confirmed with falling 30-week MA, price 16.8% below it. CAN SLIM 0/5, RS below 1.0 across all timeframes vs both SPX and sector. OBV deeply negative (-99.9M vs +9.2M average) with volume ratio 0.80 confirming institutional distribution. The orderly, low-volatility nature of the decline means it can grind further.

## CHANGE CONDITIONS
- **To upgrade to BUY**: Price must reclaim the 200-day MA ($89.17) on above-average volume, RSI must recover above 50 with OBV confirming a rising trend, and 3-month RS vs SPX must improve above 1.0. All three conditions must be met simultaneously.
- **To confirm SELL with higher conviction**: Normalized operating income declining below $2.0B annualized for two consecutive quarters, indicating structural margin compression from regulatory action or competitive price wars.

## RISK CONSIDERATIONS
- **This is the weakest SELL of the three stocks**: The fundamental underpinning is the strongest — wide moat, doubling FCF, PEG under 1.2. This stock is the most likely to reverse on any positive catalyst.
- **CAN SLIM distortion**: The Q4 2024 EPS of $3.21 included ~$6B in one-time gains. Stripping those, operational EPS is improving, which makes the 0/5 CAN SLIM score somewhat misleading.
- **Stage 4 can persist**: Despite the strong fundamentals, Stage 4 declines with falling MAs and distribution volume can continue for months. Buying into a falling knife on valuation alone is historically risky.
- **Potential selling climax**: The Feb 4 high-volume selloff (63M shares) could mark a climax, but lack of recovery volume argues against this interpretation.
