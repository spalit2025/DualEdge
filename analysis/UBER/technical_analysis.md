**Analysis:**

**RSI:** At 30.76, the RSI is one tick above the oversold threshold of 30. This is technically "near oversold" but not yet a buy signal. RSI readings of 30-35 during Stage 4 declines often persist for weeks or even decline further. In a confirmed downtrend, oversold RSI readings indicate weakness, not necessarily buying opportunities. The risk-neutral oversold threshold is 25 -- we are above that.

**Z-Score:** At -2.39, the price is 2.4 standard deviations below its 6-month mean. This is statistically extreme and typically only seen during significant sell-offs. From a pure mean-reversion perspective, this suggests the stock is stretched to the downside and a snap-back rally is statistically probable at some point. However, Z-score analysis works best in range-bound markets and can produce misleading signals during trending markets. In Stage 4 declines, what appears "extremely cheap" can get cheaper.

**200-Day MA Distance:** The stock is 17.1% below its 200-day MA of $89.17. The risk-neutral threshold for "extended" is 20%, so UBER has not yet reached the extreme extension level -- but it is approaching it. If the decline continues another 3-5%, this threshold will be breached.

**Synthesis of Mean Reversion Signals:** There is genuine mean-reversion tension here. The Z-score is extreme, RSI is near oversold, and the stock is approaching the 200d MA extension limit. These signals suggest a counter-trend bounce is increasingly probable. HOWEVER, mean reversion is a tactical (days-to-weeks) signal, while the Stage 4 structural downtrend is a strategic (weeks-to-months) signal. Mean reversion signals during downtrends produce tradable bounces, not trend reversals. The bounce, if it comes, is more likely to stall at the falling 50-day MA ($83.08) or the lower end of the gap zone.

**Verdict: TACTICALLY NEUTRAL / STRUCTURALLY BEARISH.** The mean reversion indicators suggest a bounce is increasingly likely, but the structural downtrend is intact. For a risk-neutral investor with a multi-week horizon, the mean reversion signal is not sufficient to override the bearish trend signals from frameworks 1-5.
