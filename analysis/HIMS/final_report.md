## AGREEMENTS
- **Revenue growth is genuine and impressive**: Both analysts confirm 49-69% YoY revenue growth, with Q3 2025 at $599M. The business is growing rapidly.
- **The first profitable year (FY2024) was a milestone**: Both recognize the transition from losses to profitability as meaningful.
- **Insider selling is a significant red flag**: Both analysts flag the unanimous, heavy selling by all named insiders with zero purchases as a material negative signal.
- **EPS deterioration is concerning**: Both note the sharp decline from $0.32 (Q3 2024) to $0.06 (Q3 2025) despite strong revenue growth, indicating margin compression.

## DISAGREEMENTS
- **Value Analyst (BUY 6/10)**: PEG of 0.3-1.4 is attractive for a company growing revenue at 50%. Forward P/E of 16.4x is compelling. F-Score of 6/9 shows improving fundamentals. The 72% drawdown and analyst target of $31 (69% upside) create asymmetric upside potential. However, the analyst acknowledged this as a "marginal" BUY call.
- **Technical Analyst (SELL 8/10)**: All six technical frameworks produce SELL — the only stock where mean reversion also defaults to SELL. Stage 4 with price 54% below the 30-week MA. CAN SLIM 0/5. RS vs SPX at 0.34 (severe laggard). RSI 18.3 deeply oversold but in a confirmed downtrend this is a symptom, not a reversal signal. Volume confirms distribution with 10x normal volume on recent down days.

## CHANGE CONDITIONS
- **To upgrade to BUY**: Weekly close above the 30-week MA ($42.33) on 2x+ average volume, RSI recovery above 50, and at least two consecutive quarters of EPS re-acceleration above $0.20. Stock would need to more than double from current levels to meet the MA condition.
- **To confirm SELL with higher conviction**: Quarterly EPS declining below breakeven for two consecutive quarters, gross margins compressing below 70%, or sequential revenue decline. The EPS trajectory is trending toward triggering this within 1-2 quarters.

## RISK CONSIDERATIONS
- **This is the highest-conviction SELL of the three stocks**: Unlike AMZN and UBER where strong fundamentals contradict the technical damage, HIMS has fundamental warning signs that reinforce the bearish technical picture.
- **Regulatory risk is material and imminent**: Heavy reliance on compounded GLP-1 medications faces ongoing FDA scrutiny. A regulatory crackdown could eliminate a meaningful revenue stream.
- **Debt explosion**: From $11M to $1.12B in 9 months for unproven acquisitions (peptide manufacturing, lab testing) adds execution risk during a period of profitability deterioration.
- **Bounce risk (for shorts)**: RSI at 18.3 and 72% drawdown create conditions for violent short-covering rallies. A 20-30% dead-cat bounce to $23-25 is plausible but would not change the intermediate-term bearish thesis.
- **Potential bull catalyst**: A dramatic earnings beat, regulatory clarity on compounding, or acquisition offer could produce a violent upside move given the extreme oversold condition. However, none of these are visible in current data.
