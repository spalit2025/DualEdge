LITE is in a textbook, high-confidence Stage 2 advance: the 30-week MA ($507.87) is rising, price ($903.80 last close) is firmly above it, and weekly volume has been expanding through the markup. The one caveat is *maturity* — a price-to-30wk-MA ratio of 1.78 is very late-stage. Stage 2 advances that stretch this far above the trend line tend to be closer to the end than the beginning, and the eventual transition to Stage 3 (top) is usually marked by the first weekly close back below the 30-week MA. For a risk-neutral buyer, Stage 2 is still a "BUY" stage — but this is a "ride the existing trend with a tight stop," not a "fresh breakout from a base" setup.

### Synthesis

**Where the frameworks agree:** Trend is up and the stock is a leader. Weinstein (Stage 2), 6m/12m relative strength (top-percentile), and the current-quarter earnings inflection all point the same direction. No framework says the trend has *broken*.

**Where they conflict:** The trend-following frameworks (Weinstein, long-horizon RS) say BUY; the mean-reversion and short-horizon momentum frameworks say CAUTION. Price is +126% above its 200-day MA, 1-month RS has gone negative against SPX, XLK *and* SOXX, and volume is leaning slightly toward down days. The bull case is "this is a Stage 2 leader, ride it"; the bear case is "this is a parabola in its late innings, and the first crack will be expensive."

**Resolution (risk-neutral):** BUY, but only 5/10 conviction — the trend earns the "BUY," the extreme extension and deteriorating short-term internals cap the confidence. A risk-neutral entry here demands a hard stop (a weekly close below the 30-week MA at ~$508 is the structural line; a tighter mental stop near the 50-day MA at ~$788 limits the drawdown) and a willingness to take partial profits if RSI spikes >70 into a lower high. This is a "trade the trend with discipline," not a "back up the truck" technical setup.
