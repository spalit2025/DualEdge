## AGREEMENTS
- **Amazon's moat is unquestionable**: Both analysts agree the business has a wide economic moat across e-commerce, AWS, and advertising with pricing power, switching costs, network effects, and cost advantages.
- **The $132B capex cycle is the key variable**: Both identify the unprecedented 2025 capex spend as the central uncertainty — the Value Analyst views it as a growth investment, the Technical Analyst views the market as skeptical of returns.
- **The stock is deeply oversold**: RSI at 27.8 and Z-score of -2.34 are extreme readings. Both analysts acknowledge short-term bounce potential.
- **Insider selling is routine, not alarming**: Both agree C-suite selling appears tied to 10b5-1 plans and Bezos's systematic diversification, not bearish conviction.

## DISAGREEMENTS
- **Value Analyst (BUY 6/10)**: The fundamental picture is strong — operating income surged from $12B to $80B in 3 years, PEG is 0.98, forward P/E of 22.6x is reasonable for 12% revenue growth with expanding margins, and 63 analysts unanimously recommend Buy with a $283 mean target (35% upside). The technical weakness is a buying opportunity in a fundamentally sound business.
- **Technical Analyst (SELL 7/10)**: The technical damage is severe and broad-based — Stage 3 topping, CAN SLIM 1/5, RS deteriorating across all timeframes vs both SPX and sector, distribution volume with OBV 39% below its moving average, and expanding volatility. Five of six frameworks independently produce SELL. In a broken trend structure, oversold readings signal momentum capitulation, not buying opportunities.

## CHANGE CONDITIONS
- **To upgrade to BUY**: Price must reclaim the 30-week MA ($228.20) on above-average weekly volume, with RSI recovering above 40 and RS vs SPX trajectory shifting from "deteriorating" to "improving." All three conditions must be met simultaneously.
- **To confirm SELL with higher conviction**: AWS revenue growth declining below 15% for two consecutive quarters, operating margins contracting YoY for two quarters, or capex failing to translate into incremental operating income by Q3 2026.

## RISK CONSIDERATIONS
- **Capex execution risk**: The $132B 2025 capex is the largest single-year investment in corporate history. If AI infrastructure returns disappoint, the growth premium in the stock evaporates.
- **Stage 3-to-4 transition risk**: The stock is 8.5% below the 30-week MA with distribution volume. A Stage 4 decline would imply further significant downside.
- **Mean reversion bounce risk (for shorts)**: RSI at 27.8 and Z-score of -2.34 make a short-term bounce to $220-225 highly probable. This does not change the intermediate-term thesis but creates near-term volatility.
- **Analyst consensus risk (for bears)**: Zero Sell ratings across 63 analysts with a $283 mean target. If the fundamental view proves correct, the current price represents significant upside.
