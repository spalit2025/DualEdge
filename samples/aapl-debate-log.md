## DEBATE SUMMARY

The fundamental vs. technical disagreement on AAPL is a textbook case of the oldest tension in investing: **price vs. value**. The Value Analyst correctly identifies that AAPL is trading at a significant premium to intrinsic value by traditional metrics. The Technical Analyst correctly identifies that the price trend is strong, institutionally supported, and showing no distribution signals. Neither is wrong — they are measuring different things. The tiebreaker goes to the Technical Analyst on higher confidence, but the Value Analyst's caution about limited upside (5.2% to mean analyst target) and extreme valuation premium should inform position sizing and risk management.
